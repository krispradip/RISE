# Executive Story v2 — Framework for leadership review

**Status:** Concept/storyboard, not the final presentation

## One-sentence narrative

We are shifting Customer Care from a manual, people-dependent operation towards an intelligent service model where **AI handles, assists and assures** customer interactions — and VOX Digital Concierge illustrates how that capability can extend into digital customer journeys.

## The ten-minute run of show

| Time | Scene | What the audience sees | Core message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:00–01:30 | Day in the life | One agent surrounded by systems, training, knowledge and manual QA | Understand the work involved in helping one customer |
| 01:30–02:30 | Why change? | Demand amplifies the same people-dependent tasks | This operating model is hard to scale consistently |
| 02:30–04:00 | AI Handles / Assists / Assures | Three capabilities converge around the customer | We are redesigning how Customer Care operates |
| 04:00–06:30 | Same customer, two journeys | A before-and-after scenario using relevant current / intended capabilities | Experience the transformation rather than list products |
| 06:30–07:30 | Three proof points | AI handling, QA coverage, operational or customer outcome | Ground the change in approved evidence |
| 07:30–09:00 | Beyond Customer Care | VOX Digital Concierge as a future-facing digital journey | The change can reach the customer directly |
| 09:00–10:00 | Closing | One memorable statement | We're building an intelligent service engine for MAF |

## Decisions before building the full executive experience

1. **One customer scenario:** Agree the specific interaction used to connect the manual and AI-enabled experiences.
2. **Hero demonstration format:** Live, prerecorded, or illustrative? If multiple products are shown, do not imply a live integrated handoff without verification.
3. **The actuals:** Confirm three approved metrics, including measurement period, denominator and target classification.
4. **AI Assists:** Identify which agent enablement or knowledge capabilities are live and which remain design/roadmap.
5. **VOX future:** Confirm what can truthfully be demonstrated of Digital Concierge.
6. **Stage ownership:** Presenter timings and who controls the before/after demo.

## Non-negotiable design guardrails

- Present **a transformation story**, not a catalogue of programmes.
- Keep one consistent customer context through the hero demonstration.
- Make evidence distinguishable from ambition, concept or demo simulation.
- Preserve the original `main` showcase while this branch is under review.
- Do not claim 70% AI enablement, 100% QA, or any other target as achieved.
- Finish with a single closing statement — no further demo after the last scene.

## Closing statement for discussion

> Customer Care is no longer just answering customers. We're building an intelligent service engine for MAF.
